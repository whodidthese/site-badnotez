YOUR NOTEBOOKS, YOUR CHOICES

Privacy, plainly.

Badnotes is maintained by whodidthese. This policy covers the iPad app and this website.

Updated September 30, 2026

Your notebooks stay on your iPad.

Handwriting, images, titles, covers and settings are stored in the app on your device. Badnotes requires no account and has no ads, third-party analytics or notebook cloud sync. It does not automatically send notebooks to the developer. Device backups are handled by iPadOS and your chosen backup service.

You choose what to import and share.

The system photo picker provides only the images you select. Selected images become part of your local notebook or cover. Exporting a PDF, image or editable backup uses the destination you choose. Saving an image to Photos may ask for permission to add photos. Copies shared to another app or service follow that recipient’s policies.

You control local deletion.

Deleted notebooks go to Recently Deleted, which keeps up to 30 notebooks, until restored, permanently deleted or removed as older entries make room. Temporary recovery files may remain during saving or deletion. Delete App in iPadOS removes the app and its local data; Offload App retains data. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately.

Diagnostics stay local unless you share them.

A size-limited, rotating local log records app and system versions, device model, time, operation identifiers and technical events or errors. It is not designed to record notebook titles, handwriting or photos, and is not uploaded automatically. You can view, copy, export or clear it in the app’s Log screen.

This website is simple, too.

We add no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies or browser storage. Fonts come from your device. Language is selected through the URL, with English as the default. GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in.

GitHub Pages data collection · GitHub Privacy Statement

TestFlight is handled by Apple.

If you use TestFlight, Apple shares crash and usage information with the developer. Depending on the invitation, this may include your name or email. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared through Apple. We use this information to diagnose issues, respond to feedback and improve Badnotes, and do not share it with third parties.

Support and your requests.

Contact whodidthese through GitHub Issues for support or to request access to or deletion of information you have provided. Issues are public: start with the request type and ask for a private contact method before sending sensitive content.

Contact Badnotes on GitHub

We retain support and developer-accessible beta information only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up. Apple and GitHub retain their own data under their policies. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its date.
