PRIVACY & YOUR CHOICES

Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Badnotes iPad app and this website, maintained by whodidthese. Badnotes is built around local notebooks and requires no account. Below we explain what stays on your device and what happens when you use sharing, support, or beta-testing services.

Effective / last updated: September 6, 2026

Data handled by the app

Badnotes has no registration, ads, advertising-identifier access, or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app does not automatically transmit your notebooks or its local diagnostic log to the developer. There is no Badnotes cloud server for uploading or syncing notebooks.

Apple’s TestFlight data and information you choose to provide for support are separate cases described below. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

Notebooks and local storage

Notebook titles, page order, handwriting, inserted images, covers, and related settings are stored in the app’s on-device storage for display, editing, saving, and export. Badnotes does not provide iCloud, CloudKit, or third-party cross-device sync.

Device backups and exported files you save may be handled by iPadOS, Apple, or your chosen storage service under their settings and policies; these are separate from app-level sync. We cannot access or restore notebooks on your device for you.

Photos, export, and sharing

When you insert an image or choose a cover, the system photo picker provides the items you select. The app does not request read access to your entire photo library. Selected images are processed on your device and stored as part of your notebook or cover.

You can export notebooks as PDF or images and choose a destination in the system share sheet. Saving exported images to Photos may prompt for permission to add photos; you can manage that permission in iPadOS Settings. Exports contain the notebook content you choose. Once shared to another app or service, they are handled under that recipient’s policies.

Local diagnostic log

For troubleshooting, Badnotes keeps a size-limited, rotating diagnostic log on your device. It includes the app and OS versions, device model, time and time zone, operation identifiers, and technical events or errors. It is not automatically uploaded and is not designed to record notebook titles, handwriting, or photos.

You can view, copy, export, or clear the log in the app’s Log screen. We receive a copy only if you choose to send it to us. Review it and remove sensitive information before sharing.

TestFlight and Apple services

If you test Badnotes through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash and usage information and shares it with the developer; depending on the invitation method, this may include your name or email. Feedback, comments, or screenshots you submit are also shared through Apple. We use this beta-testing information only to diagnose issues, respond to feedback, and improve Badnotes, and do not share it with third parties.

Apple handles App Store, system diagnostics, and TestFlight data under its own policies. See: TestFlight & Privacy · Apple Privacy Policy.

This website and GitHub

This website adds no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or browser local storage. The language comes from the URL or your browser preference; choosing a language only updates the URL parameter. Fonts are supplied by your device, and site assets are served from this website.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. When you visit GitHub Issues, GitHub also handles account and usage data under its own policies. See: GitHub Pages data collection · GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

You can delete notebooks in the app and clear diagnostics in the Log screen; older log entries are also replaced as the log rotates. Internal recovery files from saving or deletion may temporarily remain in app-local storage. Using Delete App in iPadOS, rather than Offload App, removes the app and its local data.

Copies exported to Files, Photos, backups, or other services are not removed with the app; manage and delete those copies at each destination. We have no server-side copy of your notebooks to delete.

Support information you provide and testing data available to the developer are retained only as needed to troubleshoot, answer requests, and handle necessary follow-up. You may request access to or deletion of copies we control using the contact below. Public GitHub discussions and data retained by Apple are also subject to those platforms’ retention policies; we cannot guarantee removal of copies held by third parties.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions, data access or deletion requests, or support, contact Badnotes maintainer whodidthese: Badnotes support on GitHub Issues.

Issues are public. Start with the type of question or request, without posting email addresses, private notes, photos, or unreviewed diagnostic logs. If private information is needed to handle your request, first ask for a suitable private contact method.

If Badnotes’ data practices change, we will update this page and the date above. Please review the current policy when using a new version.
